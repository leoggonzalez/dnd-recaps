# Session 28.10.2026 — Draft Storyboard

**In-world date:** 6 Ches 1492 DR  
**Location:** Road from Baldur's Gate to Waterdeep

This storyboard and its scene prompts are based on the session recap and available party/NPC references. Recap events are kept distinct from proposed framing. The illustrations have been generated; the selected files are listed at the end of this document.


**Animated adaptation note:** The original eight beats expand to nine app scenes following the split of the daylight wagon sequence. The current `src/model/characters/lucien.json` and portrait supersede the older bald/moustache-only descriptions below: Lucien has cropped black hair and a short trimmed beard. New animated assets follow `animated_storyboard.md` for 16:9 parchment/ink layers; existing still prompts and illustrations are preserved.

## 1. A little luck

- **Recap event:** Samardag offers the caravan a game of Three-Dragon Ante. Radecere joins with a surprisingly substantial purse; Arietta draws Lucien toward an empty seat. Sulesdeg and Tyjit watch without joining.
- **Characters:** Lucien, Arietta, Balerias, Samardag, Radecere, Sulesdeg, Tyjit.
- **Current framing (user clarification):** The caravan has stopped to rest for the night. At least ten wagons are parked in a long line, with paired draft horses, varied merchandise, and merchants and guards near the wagons. Lucien and Arietta sit together gambling with Balerias, Samardag and Radecere around a lantern-lit crate table. Sulesdeg and Tyjit stand watch. Samardag and Radecere remain indistinct and provisional. Earlier still prompts below are historical references.
- **Night assets:** [far sky/ground](../../../../public/assets/environments/camp-night-far-v1.webp), [parked merchant caravan](../../../../public/assets/environments/camp-night-merchant-line-v1.webp), [near foliage](../../../../public/assets/environments/camp-night-near-v1.webp), [seated gamblers](../../../../public/assets/groups/camp-night-gamblers-v1.webp), [watching guards](../../../../public/assets/groups/camp-night-guards-v1.webp). Earlier assets are preserved.
- **Mood:** Welcoming and lively, with a trace of watchfulness.

## 2. A walk through camp

- **Recap event:** Arietta asks Lucien about his past and bloodline magic. Lucien senses that she is concealing something.
- **Characters:** Lucien, Arietta.
- **Proposed framing:** A two-shot among the lantern-lit wagons. Arietta is warm and curious; Lucien listens with a guarded, perceptive expression.
- **Continuity:** Use Lucien's bald head, dark skin, moustache, and crimson-purple robes with gold details. Use Arietta's olive hooded cloak, blue-gray scarf, braided auburn hair, and travel clothes.

## 3. An offering for the peryton

- **Recap event:** Balerias slowly offers food to the caged peryton while Oyn stays back. The creature accepts the food and remains near him. Balerias prepares magic that could let him speak with it.
- **Characters:** Balerias, Oyn, the peryton.
- **Proposed framing:** Balerias's outstretched hand and the peryton's cautious approach are the focus; Oyn and his bird-cage rack remain a step back. A glimmer in the creature's eyes suggests the unexplained lantern-like reflection without resolving its cause.
- **Mood:** Quiet, tentative trust with an undertone of mystery.

## 4. A note in the dark

- **Recap event:** Invisible, Lucien searches Arietta and Zelina's belongings, finds a journal, and writes in it with his Emerald Pen.
- **Characters:** Lucien; Arietta and Zelina are asleep nearby but need not appear in frame.
- **Proposed framing:** A close view of the open journal, the potion, and a pen moving in otherwise empty darkness. Avoid legible generated writing; let the act of writing carry the beat.
- **Mood:** Secretive and tense, with a hint of irony in Lucien's confident accusation while he is still investigating.

## 5. Counting the wagons

- The next morning, Aldrik rides past three Cult of the Dragon wagons at a cautious distance. Six stern guards travel silently with each wagon. He watches as he passes and suspects that one might be a decoy.

## 6. Out of earshot

- Aldrik and Balerias walk away from the caravan and talk privately about his suspicions. They consider earning the cultists’ trust so they can follow if the wagons leave.

## 7. Under the horse's neck

- **Recap event:** Ettercaps and a huge spider attack the caravan horses in the tangled forest. The defenders fight back.
- **User-confirmed staging:** A long line of horse-drawn wagons advances down the center, with two conductors per wagon and Coppercoil and Lucien fighting from the lead wagon. An ettercap attacks their left draft horse and Coppercoil sends Coppergoat charging at it. Guards hold off two giant spiders farther back. Aldrik hangs sideways from his mount on the right and shoots under its neck at the ettercap.
- **Continuity:** Preserve Aldrik’s light crossbow and his established appearance. The next scene covers the ensuing rescue.

## 8. The horses fight back

- **Recap event:** The horses land unusually powerful, critical strikes against the spiders, becoming a major force in the fight. Near the end, an ettercap webs an entire horse and starts dragging it toward the forest; Lucien kills the ettercap before it can take the horse away.
- **Characters:** The horses (including Strawberry Three and Mo Two), Lucien, the ettercap, other defenders nearby.
- **Proposed framing:** Catch the rescue at its clearest instant: the horse is being hauled toward the dark tree line while Lucien's attack brings down the ettercap. In the wider scene, show other horses kicking at the spider attackers, so their decisive contribution reads clearly without competing with Aldrik's separate mounted shot.
- **Mood:** Desperate, kinetic, then abruptly relieved.

## 9. The cocoon’s secret

- Lucien kneels beside the cocoon in the same woodland and cuts it open with a knife while Coppercoil, Balerias and Aldrik look on.
- A dead woman lies inside clutching a rolled scroll. The company recovers the scroll, a silver cloak pin and seventy-six gold pieces. The scroll’s contents remain unknown.

## Open visual references

- Samardag the Hoper and Radecere Perethun have no character-card images yet; keep them visually provisional in Scene 1.
- Background caravan defenders in the forest can remain secondary unless further references are provided.

## Generation prompt drafts

These prompts are drafts for review, not approval to generate. Use the shared art direction with each scene prompt. Keep the established character appearances consistent from one image to the next.

### Shared art direction

> Create a single-frame, landscape 3:2 illustration for a cohesive high-fantasy campaign storybook. Use painterly realism, finely textured brushwork, believable anatomy and equipment, expressive but natural faces, and cinematic composition. Keep medieval-fantasy materials and caravan details grounded and tactile. Match lighting to each scene while maintaining a rich, restrained color palette. Preserve the supplied character descriptions exactly. No captions, lettering, readable writing, panel borders, game interface, watermark, modern objects, duplicated characters, extra limbs, or inconsistent weapons.

### 1. A little luck

> At dusk in a caravan camp, weary travellers gather around a small fire for Three-Dragon Ante. In the foreground, Arietta Innevar—an adult human traveller with braided auburn-brown hair, a worn olive hooded cloak, blue-gray scarf, layered cream and muted travel clothes, belt pouches, and a pack—gently guides Lucien toward an open place at the game. Lucien is a dark-skinned, bald man with a distinctive moustache, wearing dark crimson and purple robes with gold details. Beside the overturned crate used as a table, show Samardag only from behind or cropped at the shoulders, raising a coin pouch to invite the crowd; his appearance is not established. Cards and scattered coins catch the firelight. Show Radecere taking a seat with a notably substantial purse, but keep his appearance indistinct because no visual reference is available. At the edge of the gathering stand Sulesdeg the Pole, exceptionally tall and dark-skinned in layered navy-and-tan garments with a headwrap and spear, and Tyjit Skesh, a red-braided shield dwarf in chainmail and a red cloak, watching with folded arms. Lively warm firelight, social energy, a subtle sense that the guards are alert. Do not add readable card text or insignia.

### 2. A walk through camp

> A quiet two-shot along a lantern-lit caravan camp at night. Arietta Innevar walks arm-in-arm with Lucien: she is an adult human traveller with braided auburn-brown hair, a weathered olive hooded cloak, blue-gray scarf, layered cream and muted travel clothes, and a practical belt with pouches. She speaks with warm, focused curiosity. Lucien, bald with dark skin and a distinctive moustache, wears dark crimson and purple robes edged in gold; he listens closely, his expression charismatic but guarded, as if he suspects there is more behind her questions. Wagons and low camp lanterns recede softly behind them. Let their contrasting expressions carry the scene; no visible spellcasting or invented magical aura. Intimate, warm, gently suspenseful atmosphere.

### 3. An offering for the peryton

> Beside a sturdy animal cage at a caravan camp in the last light of evening, elderly ranger Balerias Chukk carefully extends food toward a wary peryton. Balerias is rugged and weathered, with long unkempt gray hair, a thick gray beard, one mechanical eye, and worn practical ranger clothes; his oversized crossbow is close at hand but lowered. The peryton has stepped forward to accept the food and remains near him, its posture cautious but less fearful. Its eyes catch an odd lantern glimmer. Depict it as a large antlered, feathered winged predator, a provisional standard peryton interpretation. Oyn Evenmor, a broad human wagon master with shoulder-length graying hair, a full gray beard, cream tunic, dark green shawl, red sash, staff, and a rack of colorful caged birds, watches from a deliberately safe distance. The focus is Balerias's careful hand and the creature's tentative trust. Quiet, intimate, faintly uncanny; do not show the creature speaking.

### 4. A note in the dark

> A close, low view inside a shadowy caravan sleeping space after everyone has gone to bed. An open travel bag contains a plain healing potion and a journal. The journal lies open while an elegant Emerald Pen writes by itself, revealing that its unseen owner is invisible. Nearby bedrolls and sleeping figures are only soft, indistinct shapes at the edge of frame; do not show or define their faces. A very faint disturbance in the air may suggest invisible movement, but no visible person or silhouette. Keep the handwriting illegible and the page free of readable words. The frame should feel hushed, secretive, and slightly audacious, lit by a thin spill of moonlight and a nearly spent camp lantern.

### 5. Counting the wagons

> On a secluded stretch of road away from the caravan's listeners, Aldrik Gauls'Thein walks beside Balerias Chukk and quietly explains his suspicions. Aldrik is a slender, pale high elf with pointed ears, short messy dark hair, no facial hair, and fitted dark leather rogue armor; he gestures back toward the road. Balerias is an elderly, weathered ranger with long gray hair and beard, one mechanical eye, practical worn gear, and a huge crossbow carried safely. Far in the background, partly obscured by trees and distance, show exactly three Cult of the Dragon wagons moving with disciplined guards: two watchers facing backward, two watching the front flanks, and one driver per wagon; suggest a sixth traveller only in the middle wagon. Keep all cultist faces indistinct and add no treasure, emblems, or specific wagon contents. A private, wary conversation in cool daylight, with the trio of wagons just visible enough to motivate their concern.

### 6. Under the horse's neck

> A dramatic action frame in a narrow forest road tangled with roots, thorny branches, and fresh webbing. Make Aldrik's mounted shot the unmistakable focal point: the horse is crossing the frame, while Aldrik rides turned sideways in the saddle, leaning carefully beneath the horse's neck and aiming his light crossbow at a huge spider. The horse's neck forms a natural foreground arch around Aldrik and the line of aim. Aldrik is a slim, pale, youthful high elf with pointed ears, short messy dark hair, no facial hair, and dark fitted leather armor. The huge spider looms among the branches ahead; show that it is his target without implying whether the bolt hits or kills it. Webbed harnesses and frightened caravan horses establish the danger. Keep other defenders small and secondary in the background. Tense, kinetic, legible staging; anatomically plausible horse and rider, no contorted pose.

### 7. The horses fight back

> In the same tangled forest battle, a powerful caravan horse is wrapped in sticky silk across its body and being dragged toward the dark tree line by an ettercap. Lucien, bald and dark-skinned with a moustache and crimson-purple robes with gold trim, hurls an object with spell-driven force into the creature, stopping it before it can carry the horse away. In the middle distance, another horse rears and lands a decisive hoof strike against a huge spider, making clear that the horses themselves are dealing serious damage. Show the other defenders only as secondary figures among webs, harnesses, and branches. The endangered horse is frightened but not visibly injured beyond the webbing. Focus on the instant of rescue and the horses' surprising counterattack; urgent, muddy, chaotic, but readable and non-graphic.

### 8. The cocoon's secret

> After the forest fight, Balerias Chukk carefully cuts open a large web cocoon as the exhausted company gathers behind him. Balerias is an elderly, rugged ranger with long gray hair, a thick beard, one mechanical eye, worn practical gear, and a huge crossbow nearby. Inside the cocoon, show only part of an unidentified dead woman's hand still clutching a scroll; keep the body mostly concealed by silk and shadow, with no graphic detail and no defined face or invented costume. The scroll is the visual center, accompanied by a small silver cloak pin and a few gold coins recovered nearby. The other figures react in subdued silence. Cool forest shade and a narrow shaft of late light make the discovery feel solemn and unresolved. No readable writing on the scroll.
