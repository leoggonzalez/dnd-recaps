# Session Recap — 04.11.2026

**In-world dates:** 7–9 Ches 1492 DR\
**Location:** Road from Baldur's Gate to Waterdeep

1. The parchment recovered from the forest’s webbed corpse finally surrendered its secret: Coppercoil identified it as a [[Spell Scroll (Tremor)]] and passed it from Balerias Chukk’s keeping to Lucien. Lucien also claimed the [[Silver cloak pin]]. That night, his dreams offered something considerably less straightforward.  He stood in a chamber of polished black stone before a familiar robed figure. News of someone sighted near a town dissolved beneath the sound of rushing water. Black liquid seeped between the tiles. The chamber became a bridge crowded with soldiers, arrows crossing the sky above a river turning dark.  Then came a reflection: a traveller wearing a woollen cap in warm sunlight. Wind lifted it, exposing intricate markings across his forehead.  “The markings! Remember the markings!”  Broken swords and forgotten graves replaced the chamber. Voices whispered of a bridge, a crossing lost, a river. Through them came one fragment—[[Dagger]]—before black water swallowed Lucien whole.  He woke tangled in blankets. Across the wagon, [[Arietta Innevar]] watched with quiet curiosity and asked whether he always dreamed so intensely. The morning offered no clear explanation.  As the caravan resumed its journey, [[Zelina Innevar]] asked Coppercoil for a lesson in magic. Her first attempt produced a thread of light and a small pop. She thought she understood the words; her hands apparently required further instruction.  Under Coppercoil’s guidance, she found the pattern of the Weave. Sparks danced between her fingers, warming her palm.  “I did that,” she said.  Coppercoil found her magic unusual: neither draconic nor fey, and nothing of necromancy about it. Its nature remained elusive. Soon Zelina tired, describing the sensation as something pulling at a thread inside her.
2. She still wanted to learn. Lowering her voice while Arietta was occupied, she revealed a drawing of a black-glass perfume vial bound with copper wire. Her mother had called the material nightglass. She hoped to make Arietta a gift, then fill it with perfume from the gold dwarf near the caravan’s front. Coppercoil did not recognise the material, but the project had begun.  Hours later, the wagons stopped before a human head protruding from the earth.  The man was alive, sunburned, filthy, and desperately thirsty. Across his forehead someone had written OATHBREAKER.  Coppercoil gave him water. [[Tyjit Skesh]] demanded they dig him free; Aldor Urnpoleshurst argued that the delay, the excavation, and the stranger himself were somebody else’s problem.  Lucien thought he recognised the forehead marking from his dream and volunteered to take responsibility. Aldrik Gauls’Thein applied a little magical friendliness to Aldor and helped sway the gathering. When objections persisted, [[Sulesdeg the Pole]] rose to his full height and announced that the caravan had decided.  Coppercoil produced Copperbear from his [[Bag of Tricks (Rust)]]. Between bear and travellers, they uncovered the man, caught him as his legs buckled, and acquired a substantial hole that would need filling.  [[Carlon Amoffel]] explained that he had promised to marry a woman, then withdrawn after discovering her family were bandits who expected him to join them. Burial had followed. Lucien believed he was withholding something, although neither he nor Aldrik found any visible clue.
3. Lucien offered him shelter in their wagon. [[Green Imsa]] supplied clothes, deftly redirecting Coppercoil whenever his fascination with her entirely green appearance threatened to become the conversation.  Rain began that afternoon. Riding Copperbear, Coppercoil discussed the cult wagons with Aldrik, who still suspected decoys. They sought out [[Losvius Longnose]], with Aldrik’s spectral hand rescuing some porcelain from Coppercoil’s arrival.  Losvius had news. During the rescue, he had slipped into [[Lasfelro the Silent]]’s wagon and found a seven-foot wooden crate surrounded by dark soil. Nothing else.  Aldrik considered this compelling evidence of a vampire. Proof remained inside the wagon.  They persuaded Losvius to investigate a cult wagon during the next substantial fight. Satisfied with a plan dependent upon another emergency, Coppercoil returned to his workbench. Aldrik sought shelter with Balerias and [[Oyn Evenmor]]. Later, Vladefix brought ale considerably improved in quantity by rainwater.  The downpour pinned the caravan in place the following day. Coppercoil worked on Rumble’s [[Goggles of Night]], while Zelina returned to the question of nightglass.  [[Lai Angesstun]] knew nothing of it. Tyjit suggested [[Samardag the Hoper]], whose porcelain trade might offer a useful connection. Passing the cult wagons, Coppercoil found their guards still holding position in the rain; one returned his wave with a nod.
4. Samardag supplied the answer: in Amn, nightglass meant obsidian.  [[Radecere Perethun]] promptly offered to gamble his lucky pendant against Coppercoil. Coppercoil lost twenty gold. Looking sufficiently miserable afterward earned him the opportunity to buy the pendant for another two hundred and fifty.  He paid happily.  Behind a blanket shielding the workbench from Arietta, Coppercoil and Zelina broke and magically mended the obsidian into a little vial. Intricate copper wire secured its stopper. When Zelina asked how she might earn money for perfume, Coppercoil gave her five gold pieces as well. She accepted, visibly moved.  After two nights of thunder and strange whistling wind, dawn revealed mushrooms carpeting the road.  A hoof crushed one.  It moaned.
5. Other caps burst beneath boots, releasing black dust and tiny cries. Aldrik, Lucien, and Coppercoil identified the growth as little shriekers joined through a shared mycelium. Recognition provided scant protection. Their combined lament swelled into unbearable grief, driving all three away from the road.  Vladefix and the other guards used earplugs and cleared a passage over three miserable hours. The wagons finally moved; those afflicted carried the sorrow into troubled dreams.  Coppercoil blamed Carlon. He had taken to calling him Sprout, apparently convinced that a man extracted from the earth had grown there. Carlon escaped the discussion by volunteering to drive, freeing Lucien to talk further about his dreams.  Late that afternoon, Aldrik returned from scouting with news of hobgoblins attacking an overturned wagon ahead. Tyjit halted the caravan and asked them to clear the danger.  With sunset approaching, Aldrik, Coppercoil, and Lucien began planning their approach. Somewhere down the road, the attack was already underway.
