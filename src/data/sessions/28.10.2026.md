# Session Recap — 28.10.2026

**In-world date:** 6 Ches 1492 DR  
**Location:** Road from Baldur's Gate to Waterdeep

Campfires crackled between the wagons as Samardag the Hoper shook a coin pouch above an overturned crate and offered the weary caravan an evening’s excitement. A few coins, a little luck, and somebody might finally make a fortune on this miserable road.

Three-Dragon Ante drew a crowd. Radecere Perethun, ordinarily a monument to quiet indifference, took his seat with gleaming eyes and a surprisingly substantial purse. Nearby, [[Sulesdeg the Pole]] leaned against his polearm while [[Tyjit Skesh]] watched with folded arms. Neither joined the game. Both gave the impression that cheating would be a regrettably brief career.

[[Arietta Innevar]] pulled Lucien toward an empty seat. She could feel that he would be lucky.

Later, she took his arm again and led him on a walk through camp. Her questions returned to his past and his magic, circling subjects he had offered little about before. When he explained that his power came through his bloodline, her fascination sharpened.

Lucien noticed. Beneath the teasing and warm curiosity, he believed Arietta was concealing something. What that something might be remained another question.

Elsewhere, Balerias Chukk approached the captive peryton with its evening meal.

[[Oyn Evenmor]] hovered at a safer distance, pointing out that the creature’s talons could remove a man’s arm. Tossing the food through the bars seemed, to him, an excellent compromise between animal care and retaining one’s fingers. He then returned enthusiastically to questions about the Sanyphillis bird and its breeding habits. Balerias’s invented species continued to enjoy a thriving scholarly reputation.

Balerias kept his attention on the cage. Slowly, carefully, he extended the food.
The peryton watched him without moving. Then its head lowered, its folded wings loosened, and it stepped forward to accept the offering. This time it remained close afterward instead of retreating into the shadows. For a moment, Balerias had the impression that its tolerance had become something more like trust.

Lantern light glimmered strangely in its eyes, but the darkness concealed whatever had caught his attention. He readied the magic that would let him speak with animals. For now, the creature’s silence remained intact.

Lucien pursued his own unanswered questions after the camp had gone to sleep.

Invisible, he slipped toward the belongings of Arietta and [[Zelina Innevar]], passing without waking either sister or Coppercoil. Inside their bag he found a [[Potion of Healing]] and a journal.

Its entries described daily happenings, but familiar names had been replaced with descriptions: “old man that smells like strawberries,” “wizard in disguise.” The journal offered evidence of careful observation without explaining its purpose.

Lucien turned to the final page. With his [[Emerald Pen]], disguising his handwriting, he added a message.

I know what you are doing.

It was a firm statement for a man still investigating precisely that.

Later on the road, Aldrik Gauls’Thein rode past the Cult of the Dragon’s three wagons several times under the pretence of guard duty. Their arrangements rewarded scrutiny. Each wagon maintained a disciplined watch: two occupants facing backward, two near the front watching the flanks, and a fifth driving. Aldrik suspected at least one person aboard each wagon held authority over the others. The middle wagon carried a sixth traveller.
He wondered whether all three wagons held treasure or whether the cultists planned to use a decoy. Aldrik rode back to Balerias, the party’s acting leader, and the two took a walk far from prying eyes to discuss what he had seen.

Drawing on his time among the cult, Balerias shared what he remembered and repeated that their destination mattered more than the contents of their wagons. Aldrik remained unconvinced. Still, they agreed that earning the cultists’ trust might give them a reason to follow if the wagons left the caravan. They left the details for further consideration.

Ahead, the road narrowed beneath increasingly tangled branches. Roots jarred the wheels. Thorns scraped the canvas. Conversations dwindled as the canopy thickened, and the horses tossed their heads in the unfamiliar quiet.

Then a horse screamed.

Webbing snapped from the trees and tightened around its neck. Another strand caught a second animal. Harnesses strained as something began hauling the horses toward the undergrowth.

Three hunched creatures emerged, all long limbs, coarse hair, and clicking mandibles. Huge spiders moved among the trees and descended through the branches.

They wanted the horses.
Lucien hurled an object into one rushing spider with enough force to kill it. Coppergoat rammed another, and Aldrik appeared from concealment to finish it with his [[Light Crossbow]]. Balerias raised his [[Heavy Crossbow]] and buried a bolt in one of the advancing ettercaps.

The creatures pressed past the defenders to bite and claw at the teams. One horse staggered under venom and tearing wounds. The horses answered with astonishing force, landing several powerful blows, including critical strikes. Strawberry Three landed a vicious kick; Mo Two struck another attacker. Their hooves played a major part in hurting the spiders, and the intended prey proved thoroughly opposed to the arrangement.

[[Orvustia Esseren]] and [[Leda Widris]] fought with their spears while Sulesdeg and Oyn joined the defence. Lucien sent another object crashing into an ettercap, and Coppercoil followed with bright darts of force. Balerias brought down his wounded target. Aldrik felled the next.

A great spider endured a succession of pounding hooves before collapsing beneath the horses it had come to eat.

Near the end of the fight, an ettercap caught an entire horse in silk and began hauling it toward the forest. Lucien killed the creature before it could carry the horse away. The defenders closed around the remaining attackers. Unstable magic struck from Lucien’s hand. Coppercoil’s luminous missiles hammered one of them. Orvustia drove her spear home, and Balerias’s final bolt ended the struggle.

When the forest fell quiet again, Balerias found a cocoon among the webs.

He cut it open. Inside lay the half-digested body of a woman, her hand still clutching [[A scroll|a scroll]]. He recovered it, and the company gathered a [[Silver cloak pin]] and seventy-six gold pieces from the grim discovery.

The woman had died holding tight to the parchment. What she had been carrying—and why she had held on to it until the end—remained hidden within it.
