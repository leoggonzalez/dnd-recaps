# Retro action-cartoon image direction

## Style scope

This direction draws on the visual language of late-1980s and 1990s television action cartoons. It combines energetic heroic fantasy, chrome-accented space adventure, and graphic superhero staging. These are related visual traditions, not one uniform style. Choose the branch that best fits the scene and blend them only when the user's setting calls for it.

The goal is to evoke a period of hand-drawn television illustration through broad art-direction traits. All characters, costumes, emblems, creatures, settings, and props should remain original to the user's material.

## Visual findings

### Mythic heroic fantasy

Use an epic, archetypal register: champions and monsters with instantly legible silhouettes, expressive heroic poses, monumental landscapes, ancient or otherworldly architecture, and boldly shaped equipment. Make the stakes feel larger than the characters while keeping faces, action, and emotional intent easy to read.

### Chrome space adventure

Build around flight, sleek original technology, reflective metal accents, vivid cosmic horizons, and strong rim lighting. Keep metal as an accent that clarifies armor or machinery; preserve expressive faces and readable anatomy so the scene remains lively rather than mechanical.

### Graphic superhero adventure

Favor clear costume shapes, strong color separation, flat graphic space, dramatic group staging, and decisive action poses. For a darker variation, use noir contrast, geometric architecture, and deep shadow shapes. Keep the image designed and legible instead of making every surface slick or highly detailed.

## Practical synthesis for image prompts

| Design dimension | Prompt for |
|---|---|
| Shape language | Distinct, readable silhouettes; broad angular or rounded forms chosen to fit the character; repeatable landmarks in clothing, armor, and equipment |
| Character posing | Clear action verbs, expressive faces, athletic heroic proportions, planted stances, or dynamic flight poses; neutral catalog poses only when requested |
| Line and shading | Confident ink-like contours, cel-animation color blocks, a few intentional shadow shapes, selective highlights, and a hand-drawn 2D television-animation finish |
| Color | Saturated jewel, primary, or complementary colors with deliberate separation; cool cosmic colors and metallic accents for space scenes; warmer earth and jewel tones for mythic scenes |
| Setting | Graphic painted backgrounds, dramatic skies, alien horizons, ancient ruins, bold city geometry, or simplified starfields; establish place without competing with the figures |
| Composition | Strong diagonals, low angles, foreground framing, readable spacing, and a clear focal hierarchy; use motion accents only when they reinforce the action |
| Finish | Clean illustration with a hint of hand-made cel-era character; optional restrained grain or film softness, never heavy distress by default |

These are practical image-prompt recommendations, not rules for every cartoon or every year. Favor visual clarity and consistency over piling on period effects.

## Three prompt variants

1. **Mythic heroic fantasy** — monumental landscapes, champion silhouettes, expressive monsters, original symbolic gear, and jewel-toned cel color blocks.
2. **Chrome space opera** — flight poses, metallic armor accents, streamlined original technology, vivid cosmic backdrops, hard rim light, bright team-color accents, and an adventurous tone.
3. **Graphic superhero adventure** — readable original costumes, flat graphic space, dramatic panel-like staging, bold complementary colors, and expressive group dynamics. For a darker mood, shift toward geometric city forms and deep shadows.

## Prompt recipe

> Original character(s) [specific action] in [setting], [emotional tone]. Late-1980s/1990s Saturday-morning action-cartoon illustration, hand-drawn 2D cel-animation feel, [variant-specific cues], bold readable silhouettes, expressive heroic posing, clean ink contours, limited cel shadows, saturated [palette], dramatic [camera angle/light], graphic painted background, clear focal hierarchy. Preserve [continuity traits]. Keep all designs original; no lettering or brand marks.

Add only the cues that fit the image. For a sequence, keep the rendering approach, palette logic, and character design landmarks consistent across prompts.

## How to use the style

Treat this as an optional visual layer. It should not change the user's story, established character facts, scene plan, or approval steps. Choose the visual branch based on the scene, keep the prompt focused on its central action, and retain the user's established design details.
